01 Introduction

Skyward Airport Tycoon (the “App”) is an Android game developed by Mridul Pramod (the “Developer”, “we”, “us”, or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information, including information stored locally on your device and information that may be collected by third-party advertising services when advertising is introduced.

By installing or using the App, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the App.

This policy applies only to Skyward Airport Tycoon and this privacy page. It does not apply to third-party websites or services that may be linked from the App or this page.

02 Information stored locally on your device

The App stores certain information locally on your Android device so that the game can function and remember your progress. This may include:

  • Game progress, such as unlocked content, upgrades, levels, and in-game achievements
  • Game settings and preferences, such as sound, music, and display options
  • Local gameplay state needed to resume a session
  • Technical information stored by the App on the device, such as the app version

This information is stored on your device and is not transmitted to a server operated by the Developer. The App does not currently provide user accounts, login, cloud saves, analytics, or payment functionality.

You can remove locally stored information by clearing the App's storage in your Android settings or by uninstalling the App. Clearing storage or uninstalling may permanently delete your game progress.

03 Advertising and Google AdMob

Current status: advertising is planned, but Google AdMob has not yet been integrated into the App. The App does not currently contain the Google Mobile Ads SDK and does not currently show third-party ads.

When AdMob is integrated, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share information automatically, as described in Google's privacy documentation. This information may include:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID (Advertising ID) and app set ID
  • IP address, which may be used to infer an approximate, coarse location
  • Ad interactions, such as which ads were shown, viewed, or clicked
  • Diagnostic information, such as crash logs, performance data, and SDK error information
  • App and device information, such as app version, operating system version, device model, language, and network type
  • Other information collected by Google's advertising SDK as described in Google's privacy documentation

Google may use this information for purposes including delivering ads, measuring ad performance, fraud prevention, and improving its services. The exact data collected and purposes may change as Google updates its SDK. For details, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Mobile Ads SDK privacy disclosure.

Update note: when advertising is integrated, this section will be revised to describe the actual SDK version, the ads implemented, the consent tool used, and any additional third-party ad partners.

04 How information is used

Information is used only for the purposes described in this policy:

  • To operate the game and save your progress and settings locally on your device
  • To display and measure advertising once AdMob is integrated
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, and invalid ad activity
  • To diagnose crashes, errors, and performance problems in the advertising SDK
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce applicable terms

We do not currently use information for analytics, profiling, or marketing beyond the advertising functionality described above. If additional features are added, this policy will be updated before they are used.

05 Third-party services and data sharing

When advertising is integrated, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may receive information through the Google Mobile Ads SDK as described in Section 3. Google acts as an independent third party and processes information under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not currently share locally stored game progress or settings with third parties, because that information remains on your device.

Information may also be disclosed only where necessary to:

  • Comply with a legal obligation, court order, or lawful government request
  • Protect the rights, safety, and security of users, the Developer, or others
  • Investigate and prevent fraud or abuse
  • Work with service providers that process information on our behalf, subject to appropriate safeguards

Other than the planned AdMob integration, the App does not currently use analytics providers, login providers, cloud storage, or payment processors.

06 Advertising personalisation and consent

Where required by applicable law, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, we will request your consent before personal data is used for personalised advertising. We intend to use a Google-compliant consent mechanism, such as Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK, when AdMob is integrated.

If you consent, ads may be personalised based on information such as your advertising ID and approximate location. If you decline, or where consent is not required, you may still see ads, but they may be non-personalised. Non-personalised ads can still use limited information for frequency capping, fraud prevention, and aggregate measurement.

You can also control advertising personalisation through your Android device settings, including resetting or deleting your advertising ID and opting out of personalised ads where your device supports it. If the App provides an in-app privacy or consent setting after advertising is integrated, you can use that setting to review or change your choices.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, or to object to certain processing. You can contact us using the details in Section 11. You can also contact Google directly about data processed by its advertising services.

07 Data retention and deletion

Information stored locally by the App remains on your device until you clear the App's storage or uninstall the App. The Developer does not maintain a server-side account or database containing your game progress.

Information processed by Google AdMob is retained by Google according to Google's own retention policies and settings. We do not control how long Google retains that information. Please refer to Google's privacy policy for details.

Because the App does not currently have user accounts or a server-side profile, we may not be able to identify information associated with you. If you contact us about deletion, please include enough information for us to understand your request. We will take reasonable steps to assist, including directing you to the relevant Google controls where appropriate.

08 Children's privacy

Skyward Airport Tycoon is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If advertising is integrated, it will be configured in a manner consistent with applicable child-directed advertising requirements where the App is directed to children in a particular region. We will update this policy and the App's store disclosures before any such advertising is enabled.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us at mridulpramod8@gmail.com. We will review the request and take reasonable steps to address it.

09 Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information handled in connection with the App. Locally stored game data is protected by your device's security, so you should keep your device updated and use a screen lock.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, and you use the App at your own risk. If you believe your information has been compromised, please contact us using the details below.
