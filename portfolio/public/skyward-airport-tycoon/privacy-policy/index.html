01 Introduction

Skyward Airport Tycoon (the “App”) is an Android game developed by Mridul Pramod (the “Developer”, “we”, “us”, or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information, including information stored locally on your device and information collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's consent services included in the App.

By installing or using the App, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the App.

This policy applies only to Skyward Airport Tycoon and this privacy page. It does not apply to third-party websites or services that may be linked from the App or this page.

02 Information stored locally on your device

The App stores certain information locally on your Android device so that the game can function and remember your progress. This may include:

  • Game progress, such as unlocked content, upgrades, levels, and in-game achievements
  • In-game currency balances, including coins earned through the rewarded advertisement feature
  • Game settings and preferences, such as sound, music, and display options
  • Local gameplay state needed to resume a session
  • Technical information stored by the App on the device, such as the app version

This information is stored on your device and is not transmitted to a server operated by the Developer. The App does not provide user accounts, login, cloud saves, or payment functionality. Advertising-related analytics are performed by Google through the Mobile Ads SDK as described in Sections 3 and 4.

You can remove locally stored information by clearing the App's storage in your Android settings or by uninstalling the App. Clearing storage or uninstalling may permanently delete your game progress.

03 Advertising and Google AdMob

Current status: the App includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK through its Capacitor plugin, version 8.2.1, and uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent management. Advertising is currently limited to Google test ad units. Real advertising has not been enabled. At app launch, UMP refreshes consent information, required consent messages are displayed, and the App requests ads only when UMP permits. Ad availability is not guaranteed.

Google's current Play data disclosure for the Google Mobile Ads SDK states that the SDK may automatically collect and share the following information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. These practices apply to the integrated SDK, including the current test configuration, unless a different configuration is documented here.

Data the SDK may collect and share automatically
Data category What may be collected Purposes
IP address and approximate location The device's IP address, which may be used to estimate the general location of the device. Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Product interactions User product interactions and interaction information, including app launches, taps, and video views. Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Diagnostics Information about the performance of the App and the SDK, including app launch time, hang rate, and energy usage. Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Device and account identifiers The Android advertising ID, app set ID, and, if applicable, other identifiers related to signed-in accounts on the device. Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention

Ad format and reward: the App offers optional rewarded video ads. A rewarded ad that completes successfully awards 500 in-game coins. When the reward is earned, the App credits it to the local coin balance stored on your device. The reward is credited only after the ad is completed successfully.

Ad availability is not guaranteed. Ads may fail to load, be unavailable in your region, or be limited by consent choices, network conditions, ad inventory, or Google's systems. If no rewarded ad is available or the ad does not complete successfully, no reward is granted. During testing, the video is a Google test ad, not a real advertiser ad.

These collection and sharing activities are performed by Google's advertising SDK, not by a server operated by the Developer. The Developer does not receive this data into a developer-operated backend, although Google may provide aggregated advertising and performance reports. Google and its advertising partners may process the information as independent third parties under their own privacy policies.

According to Google, data collected by the Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS). The exact data collected and purposes may change as Google updates its SDK or as ad formats and mediation partners are added.

For details, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services, Google's Google Play data disclosure for the Mobile Ads SDK, and the Google Privacy Policy.

Test configuration: only Google test ad units are configured. When real advertising is enabled, this section will be updated with the production ad unit details, any mediation or third-party ad partners, and the final consent configuration.

04 How information is used

The App uses local information to operate the game. The integrated Google Mobile Ads SDK uses information only for the purposes described below. The Developer and Google act independently: the Developer controls the App and its local data, while Google controls the data its SDK collects and shares.

Processing by the Developer

  • To operate the game and save your progress and settings locally on your device
  • To credit the 500-coin reward after a rewarded ad is completed successfully and save the updated balance locally
  • To respond to privacy questions and requests sent to the contact address
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce applicable terms

Processing by Google through the Mobile Ads SDK

Google states that the integrated SDK may collect and share information for the following purposes:

  • Advertising and marketing — to serve, cap, measure and improve ads, including personalised ads where consent allows
  • Analytics — to understand App and SDK performance, including app launches, taps, video views, launch time, hang rate and energy usage
  • Fraud prevention, security and compliance — to detect invalid traffic, abuse and security issues

The Developer does not receive the SDK's raw data into a developer-operated backend. Google may provide aggregated reports about ad performance and revenue.

Test-ad status: the SDK is active with Google test ad units, and UMP controls whether ad requests are permitted. Real advertiser ads are not being served, but the data practices described in this section apply to the test configuration and will continue to apply when real ads are enabled.

05 Third-party services and data sharing

Collected and shared with Google. The integrated Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects the information described in Section 3 and shares it with Google and its advertising partners for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. This applies to the current test configuration as well as to real ads once enabled. Google acts as an independent third party and processes that information under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

The categories shared through the SDK can include IP address and approximate location, product interactions, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID. Google may share this information with advertisers, measurement providers and other partners involved in delivering and measuring ads. See Google's Google Play data disclosure for the Mobile Ads SDK and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

The Developer does not sell personal information for money and does not operate a server that receives your game progress. Locally stored progress and settings stay on your device and are not shared with third parties by the Developer. The SDK sharing described above is performed by Google, not by the Developer. Some US state laws may treat sharing for personalised advertising as “sharing” or a “sale”; you can opt out as described in Section 6.

Information may also be disclosed only where necessary to:

  • Comply with a legal obligation, court order, or lawful government request
  • Protect the rights, safety, and security of users, the Developer, or others
  • Investigate and prevent fraud or abuse
  • Work with service providers that process information on the Developer's behalf, subject to appropriate safeguards

Other than Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK, the App does not use analytics providers, login providers, cloud storage, or payment processors. If additional services or mediation partners are added, this section will be updated before they are used.

06 Advertising personalisation, consent and your choices

Where required by law, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent tool to request your consent before personal data is used for personalised advertising. The App honours the choices you make through its consent message and privacy options.

If you consent, ads may be personalised using information such as your advertising ID, approximate location and product interactions. If you decline, or where consent is not required, you may still see ads, but they should be non-personalised. Non-personalised ads can still use limited information for frequency capping, fraud prevention, and aggregate measurement.

Rewarded video ads are optional. If you decline personalised advertising, a rewarded ad may still be available in a non-personalised form. The 500-coin reward is credited only after the rewarded video is completed successfully.

Controls available to you

  • In-app consent or privacy settings — where the App presents a consent message or privacy options, you can use them to review or change your advertising choices.
  • Google Ads Settings — you can manage personalisation and opt out of personalised Google ads at adssettings.google.com.
  • Android advertising ID — in your device settings, you can reset your advertising ID or delete it where your Android version supports that control. Deleting the ID limits its use for advertising.
  • Device ad settings — you can opt out of personalised advertising in your device's Google settings where that option is available.

EEA, UK and Switzerland: you can withdraw or change consent at any time using the in-app privacy option or the controls above. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that already took place before withdrawal.

United States: if you live in a state with an applicable privacy law, you may have the right to opt out of the sharing of your information for personalised advertising. You can exercise that choice through the in-app privacy controls where offered, through Google Ads Settings, and by using your device's advertising ID controls.

Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, or to object to certain processing. You can contact the Developer using the details in Section 11. For information processed by Google's advertising services, you can also contact Google directly.

07 Data retention and deletion

Local data. Information stored locally by the App remains on your device until you clear the App's storage or uninstall the App. You can delete it at any time using your Android settings (for example, Settings → Apps → Skyward Airport Tycoon → Storage → Clear storage) or by uninstalling the App.

No developer account or server database. The App does not create user accounts and the Developer does not maintain a server-side profile or database containing your game progress. There is therefore no developer-held account to delete.

Advertising data. Information collected and shared by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is retained by Google according to Google's own retention policies and your Google account and device settings. This applies to the current test configuration as well as to real ads once enabled. The Developer does not control how long Google retains that information. See the Google Privacy Policy for details.

Deletion requests. Because the Developer does not hold a server-side profile, a deletion request will usually relate to local data you can remove yourself or to data held by Google. If you contact us about deletion, please describe your request. We will take reasonable steps to assist, including confirming what the Developer holds and directing you to the relevant Google controls where appropriate. You can also use Google's privacy controls and the advertising ID controls described in Section 6.

08 Children's privacy

Skyward Airport Tycoon is intended for a general audience and is not primarily directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). The Developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Advertising is currently limited to Google test ads. If real advertising is enabled, it will be configured consistently with applicable child-directed advertising requirements. If the App is listed for a child audience or otherwise treated as child-directed, the advertising SDK will be configured for child-directed treatment, personalised advertising will be disabled, and ad content will be limited to an appropriate rating. The Developer will also keep the Google Play target audience and content settings, the ads declaration and the Data safety section consistent with that configuration.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us at mridulpramod8@gmail.com. We will review the request and take reasonable steps to address it.

09 Security

The Developer uses reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information handled in connection with the App. Locally stored game data is protected by your device's security, so you should keep your device updated and use a screen lock.

According to Google, information collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS). The Developer does not operate the servers that receive SDK data and cannot control Google's security measures beyond relying on Google's published practices.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. The Developer cannot guarantee absolute security, and you use the App at your own risk. If you believe your information has been compromised, please contact us using the details in Section 11.
